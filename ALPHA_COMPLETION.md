```text

```

At that terminal boundary:

- implementation authority is closed;
- no next phase is authorized;
- merge authority remains human-controlled;
- the completed Alpha evidence remains part of the authority chain;
- no later phase is implied.

## What Alpha completion means

Alpha completion means the explicitly authorized Alpha lifecycle reached its terminal control state after implementation, adversarial validation, evidence admission, and human-controlled authority transitions.

## What Alpha completion does not mean

It is not a claim of:

- production readiness;
- regulatory compliance;
- certification;
- formal verification;
- universal security;
- authorization of a later development phase.
