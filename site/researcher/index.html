Research question

As autonomous systems gain access to tools, credentials, physical devices and financial infrastructure, the ability to generate a plausible answer is not proof of permission. The research asks whether an action was authorized under the correct and current conditions, whether the realized effect matched the admitted operation, and whether an independent reviewer can reconstruct the evidence chain.

Projects and responsibilities

  • Ground Truth: current authority, delegation, policy, revocation and protected-effect boundaries.
  • HERCULES: governed multi-model engineering runtime and operator interface.
  • MobiPute: distributed Android computation, deterministic verification and provenance.
  • TruthPute: requester/operator workflow and presentation of compute evidence.
  • BBAR: retained and independently reviewable audit evidence.

These are distinct engineering workstreams at different qualification stages. Their shared architectural intent does not establish that the full chain is currently deployed or pilot-qualified.

Published papers and evidence

  • Ground Truth: Deterministic Falsification of Authority Continuity in Autonomous Systems — research preprint v1.0, including canonical PDF, source and checksums.
  • Ground Truth for PHI: Deterministic Authorization of Semantic Disclosure — separately bounded post-freeze extension.
  • Public GitHub research exhibit — documented models, limitations, fixtures and provenance.
Scope and disclosure: the research exhibit intentionally excludes nonpublic runtime internals, credentials and protected operational evidence. Test results are scoped to their published versions. A security invariant is a requirement to enforce and test, not a universal guarantee.
