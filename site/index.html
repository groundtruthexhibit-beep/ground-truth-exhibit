System model

Authority, execution and operations stay separate.

The stack deliberately separates current permission from governed execution and from the operator interface. Identity alone is not authority, and verification alone is not permission.

01 / AUTHORITY

Ground Truth

Current-authority plane. Establishes permission, revocation, currentness and fail-closed outcomes for the exact action and control state.

02 / EXECUTION

MobiPute

Governed execution, independent verification, durable evidence and settlement-execution infrastructure.

03 / OPERATIONS

TruthPute

Operator and integration application. It presents jobs, results and evidence while consuming authority from Ground Truth and execution evidence from MobiPute.

CURRENT AUTHORITY → GOVERNED EXECUTION → INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION → DURABLE EVIDENCE → OPERATOR VISIBILITY → PROTECTED EFFECT
