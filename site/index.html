The engineering system

Five roles. Different trust boundaries. One coherent aim.

An AI assistant, an authority verifier, a physical compute worker and an audit record should never be allowed to impersonate one another. The combined architecture is being integrated and qualified; the diagram of responsibilities is not proof that every interface is live.

01 / OPERATOR & AI RUNTIME

HERCULES

The wolf's operator console and multi-model engineering runtime. Models can reason and propose; protected tools require a separately established, current authority path. Provider and tool integration remain subject to live qualification.

02 / AUTHORITY

Ground Truth

Root authority, delegation, revocation, policy and exact protected-effect boundaries. Evidence and model agreement cannot create their own authorization. Ambiguous required authority fails closed.

03 / COMPUTATION

MobiPute

Distributed Android execution, job provenance, deterministic verification and governed admission, with financial settlement logic kept separate. A five-device physical rehearsal is a qualification goal, not a completed pilot.

04 / OPERATOR EVIDENCE

TruthPute

Requester and operator experience for jobs, results and evidence, including planned HERCULES presentation. A displayed result is not itself an independent verification or an authority source.

05 / RETAINED EVIDENCE

BBAR

A black-box evidence plane intended for independent audit, retention, custody and recovery. Evidence may explain an action afterward; recording it does not retroactively authorize the action.

Optional settlement/evidence rail — Solana: development and devnet receipt work remain separate from the authority source. Mainnet payouts are disabled for the initial nonfinancial rehearsal. On-chain visibility does not repair missing authorization or an unfinalized transaction.

Hostile conditions

Built around the moment a proposal tries to become an effect.

The architecture is designed to contain failure modes that conventional model-first systems can blur together. Coverage for any particular attack requires an exact implementation, an adversarial test and observed evidence; a design rule alone is not a security guarantee.

Prompt or tool manipulation

Model output, injected instructions, substituted connectors and self-reported permission must not be treated as trusted authorization.

Stale and replayed authority

Revocation, changed policy, expired grants, copied receipts and rollback need evaluation at the effect-consuming boundary.

Unverified results

Worker claims, mismatched provenance or unverifiable computation must not mutate a balance merely because a job completed.

Premature financial effects

Settlement eligibility, ledger commitment and finalized on-chain outcomes are separate requirements, not interchangeable statuses.

Ambiguous recovery

Restarts, duplicates, lost acknowledgments and uncertain external outcomes must not silently create additional authority or payouts.

Unaccountable history

Evidence identity, independent custody, retention and reconstruction are needed before claims can be defended after a failure.
