Capability ≠ Evidence ≠ Authority ≠ Execution

Summary

Ground Truth studies authority continuity in autonomous systems as a deterministic falsification problem. The framework separates capability, evidence, authority, and execution; distinguishes AUTHORIZED, DENIED, and UNAVAILABLE; and tests whether the evidence required to justify a protected transition remains current and applicable at the authority-consuming execution boundary.

A central finding of the frozen v1 model is that local currentness does not by itself establish globally current authority. The public release therefore binds authority evaluation to exact state, evidence relationships, identity and boundary conditions, consumption eligibility, and a protected T3 execution event.
