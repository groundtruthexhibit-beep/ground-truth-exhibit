## Catalog boundary

The catalog records repository-verifiable evidence only. Exact branch names are
omitted where the commit and artifact are the stronger stable identifier. A
Level 7 entry means an exact-state hostile review was recorded for that slice;
it does not mean the underlying security property is proven universally.
